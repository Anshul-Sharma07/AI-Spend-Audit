# Economics

This MVP works best as a lead-generation and diagnostic product for AI cost optimization.

## Funnel Model

Example monthly funnel:

| Step | Assumption | Count |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Landing visitors | - | 2,000 |
| Start audit | 25% | 500 |
| Complete audit | 60% | 300 |
| Meaningful savings found | 50% | 150 |
| Submit lead form | 25% of completions | 75 |
| Qualified leads | 40% of leads | 30 |
| Sales calls | 30% of qualified leads | 9 |
| Customers | 20% of calls | 2 |

## CAC Assumptions

Founder-led and content-led CAC should be low early, but not zero.

| Channel | Cost Basis | Expected CAC |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Founder outbound | Time-heavy, low cash | $100 to $300 |
| LinkedIn/content | Compounding, slow | $50 to $250 |
| Paid search | Competitive but focused | $300 to $900 |
| Partner referrals | Revenue share | 10% to 20% of first deal |

Early-stage assumption: use founder-led distribution until there is evidence of conversion.

## Lead Value

Lead value depends on whether the business is self-serve software, services, or a hybrid.

Possible monetization:

1. Implementation service
   One-time fee to reduce AI spend, clean up seats, and set policies.

2. Monthly monitoring
   Lightweight subscription to review spend and tool overlap quarterly.

3. Vendor/procurement advisory
   Help teams negotiate AI subscriptions or consolidate tools.

Example lead value:

| Variable | Assumption |
| --- | ---: |
| Qualified lead to customer | 10% |
| Average first contract | $1,000 |
| Gross margin | 70% |
| Expected gross profit per qualified lead | $70 |

If the service expands to $2,500 average first contract, expected gross profit per qualified lead rises to about $175.

## Path to Profitability

Spreadsheet-style logic:

```text
Monthly visitors = 2,000
Audit completion rate = 15% of visitors
Completed audits = 300
Lead conversion = 25%
Leads = 75
Qualified lead rate = 40%
Qualified leads = 30
Close rate = 10%
Customers = 3
Average first contract = $1,000
Revenue = $3,000
Gross margin = 70%
Gross profit = $2,100
Monthly infra/tools = $150 to $300
Founder-led CAC cash cost = low
```

The product becomes interesting if it can repeatedly generate qualified leads from teams with $500+ monthly AI spend.

## Main Economic Risks

- The average user may have too little AI spend to justify paid help.
- Users may take the free recommendations and never convert.
- Savings recommendations may need more confidence before finance teams act.
- Paid acquisition may not work unless the landing page converts strongly.

## Best Economic Wedge

Do not sell to everyone using ChatGPT. Focus on teams with:

- Multiple paid AI tools
- At least 5 seats
- Engineering-heavy workflows
- A visible monthly savings number above $250

That segment has enough pain for a follow-up conversation.
