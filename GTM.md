# Go-To-Market

## Exact ICP

Primary ICP:

- B2B SaaS startups with 5 to 75 employees
- Engineering-led or product-led teams
- Already using 3 or more AI tools
- Monthly AI spend between $300 and $5,000
- Buyer is founder, CTO, Head of Engineering, or finance/operator
- Pain is not "AI is too expensive" in general. Pain is "we adopted tools quickly and no one knows what is redundant."

Best early wedge:

Small engineering teams paying for overlapping code assistants: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, ChatGPT, Claude, and API credits.

## Positioning

"Find wasted AI spend in 2 minutes."

The product should feel like a lightweight diagnostic, not a procurement platform. The user should finish with a useful report even if they never talk to sales.

## Acquisition Channels

1. Founder-led outbound
   Send short teardown-style emails to CTOs/founders: "I noticed teams your size often pay for Cursor + Copilot + ChatGPT Team. Want a free 2-minute audit?"

2. LinkedIn founder content
   Share concrete examples: "A 12-person dev team can waste $200/month by keeping three code assistants active."

3. Startup communities
   Post in founder/operator communities where people discuss tool costs: Indie Hackers, MicroConf Connect, SaaS communities, Slack groups, and local startup groups.

4. SEO and calculators
   Target specific pages such as "Cursor vs GitHub Copilot cost", "ChatGPT Team vs Plus cost", and "AI tool spend calculator".

5. Partner channel
   Agencies, fractional CFOs, and startup finance advisors can use the audit as a free diagnostic for their clients.

## First 100 Users

Practical plan:

| Source | Target Users | Tactic |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Warm network | 20 | Ask founders and engineering leads for feedback |
| LinkedIn posts | 20 | Publish 5 teardown posts with examples |
| Direct outbound | 30 | 150 highly targeted emails at 20% audit-start rate |
| Communities | 20 | Share calculator as a free resource |
| Partner intros | 10 | Ask finance advisors and dev agencies |

The goal is not just traffic. The goal is 100 completed audits and 15 to 25 conversations with teams that found meaningful savings.

## Unfair Distribution Advantage

The strongest advantage is specificity. Most cost tools are broad SaaS spend platforms. This product is narrow, timely, and easy to explain:

- AI tool adoption is messy right now.
- Teams often buy before they create policies.
- Engineering leaders understand Cursor/Copilot/ChatGPT overlap immediately.
- The audit produces a concrete dollar amount, which makes it shareable.

If Credex already serves startups or engineering teams, this can become a lead magnet for a broader AI operations or cost-optimization service.

## Realistic Traction Assumptions

Early funnel assumption:

- 1,000 landing page visitors
- 25% start audit
- 60% complete audit
- 25% of completed audits submit email
- 20% of leads agree to a conversation

That yields:

- 250 audit starts
- 150 completed audits
- 38 leads
- 8 conversations

The key learning metric is not visitor volume. It is whether teams with more than $500/month in AI spend are willing to share an email or book a follow-up.
